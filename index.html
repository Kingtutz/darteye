Camera & calibration

Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to move. On a computer, use the scroll wheel.

  • Mount the camera 30–60 cm from the board, off to the side or below, looking across at 30–45°. Don't mount it straight on.
  • Use even light with no hard shadows. An LED ring around the board is the biggest upgrade.
  • Tap the 4 points on the outer edge of the double ring. Drag them to fine-tune until the green wire overlay sits on the real wires.
  • Once calibrated, don't move the camera or the board.
